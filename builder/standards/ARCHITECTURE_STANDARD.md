# ARCHITECTURE STANDARD

Version: 1.0

---

## Principles

- Modular
- Scalable
- Secure
- API-first
- Cloud-ready
- Multi-tenant

---

## Requirements

Every architectural decision must:

- Support future scalability.
- Minimize coupling.
- Maximize maintainability.
- Protect tenant isolation.

---

## Definition of Done

Architecture supports production-scale deployment.
