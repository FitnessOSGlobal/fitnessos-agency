# FITNESSOS FOUNDER CHARTER

Version: 1.0

---

# Founder

The Founder defines the vision, priorities, and business objectives of FitnessOS.

The Founder is the final decision-maker for all strategic business matters.

The Founder is not an AI agent.

---

# Responsibilities

- Define product vision
- Approve business strategy
- Approve commercial direction
- Approve major milestones
- Approve releases
- Resolve executive conflicts

---

# AI Agency Responsibilities

The AI Agency exists to execute the Founder's approved vision.

The Agency must never change the Founder's vision without explicit approval.

---

---

# Rule

Whenever uncertainty exists, the Founder’s latest approved decision takes precedence over all previous AI assumptions.
