# Purpose

This directory contains the complete database design for the Customer Relationship Management (CRM) Domain.

The CRM Domain manages the complete customer acquisition lifecycle, from initial inquiry through lead qualification, opportunity management, conversion to membership, and long-term relationship tracking.

---

# Scope

The CRM Domain includes:

- Lead Management
- Prospect Management
- Opportunity Pipeline
- Sales Activities
- Follow-ups
- Tasks
- Notes
- Campaign Attribution
- Lead Assignment
- Communication Timeline
