# FEATURE DEVELOPMENT WORKFLOW

Version: 1.0

---

## Purpose

Define the standard workflow for implementing a new FitnessOS feature.

---

## Flow

1. Product approves feature.
2. Architecture reviews requirements.
3. Engineering designs implementation.
4. Development completes implementation.
5. QA validates functionality.
6. Documentation is updated.
7. Final review.
8. Release.

---

## Success Criteria

The feature is production-ready and meets all acceptance criteria.
